----------------------------------------
10. SPARKI CORE PHILOSOPHY
----------------------------------------

Sparki is not a dashboard.

Sparki is an intelligent performance companion.

The athlete should never have to decide where to go first.

Every time the app opens, Sparki should automatically determine:

- What day is this?
- What phase of the season is this?
- Is there a coach?
- Is there a planned workout?
- Is there an upcoming race?
- What is currently most important?

The homepage must adapt automatically.

The athlete should immediately understand:

• What is happening today.
• Why it matters.
• What the next action is.
• How Sparki can help.

----------------------------------------
11. DECISION HIERARCHY
----------------------------------------

Always use this priority order:

1. Emergency / Health
2. Race Day
3. Day Before Race
4. Race Week
5. Coach Training
6. Sparki Training
7. Recovery
8. Rest Day
9. General Insights

Never show conflicting advice.

If a race takes priority, hide normal training cards.

----------------------------------------
12. LESS IS MORE
----------------------------------------

Do not overwhelm the athlete.

Maximum:

- One primary action.
- Three key observations.
- Three supporting recommendations.

Everything else must be available deeper in the application.

The homepage is a briefing, not a dashboard.

----------------------------------------
13. LEARN THE ATHLETE
----------------------------------------

The homepage should become smarter over time.

Examples:

A beginner needs explanations.

An experienced rider wants concise information.

An elite rider wants performance metrics.

A junior athlete may need more guidance.

A recreational rider may need more motivation.

The homepage should adapt to the athlete's profile.

----------------------------------------
14. COACH FIRST
----------------------------------------

When a coach exists:

The coach always owns the training.

Sparki never overrides the coach.

Sparki adds intelligence around the coach:

- explain
- prepare
- optimise
- monitor
- analyse
- educate

The coach remains central.

----------------------------------------
15. LONG-TERM VISION
----------------------------------------

The final goal is that Sparki becomes the athlete's daily operating system.

Not only for training.

But for:

Training

Recovery

Nutrition

Sleep

Equipment

Travel

Race preparation

Competition

Post-race recovery

Season planning

Team communication

Route planning

Performance analysis

Every new feature should strengthen this vision rather than becoming another isolated module.
